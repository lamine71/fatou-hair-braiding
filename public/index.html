Good to know

Before your appointment

Deposit

$50 for styles over 3 hours, $30 for shorter styles. It comes off your final price.

Cancel or reschedule

Give at least 24 hours' notice and your deposit carries over to your new time. With less notice, the deposit is kept.

Please let us know. After 15 minutes we may have to move your appointment.

Questions

Asked and answered

How should I come prepared?
Come with your hair washed, fully detangled and stretched (blow-dried), with no product build-up. That lets us start on time.
Is braiding hair included?
Yes. Braiding hair is included in every style, so you don't need to buy or bring any.
How long will my appointment take?
Each style shows its usual time when you book. Long styles like small knotless can run 5 hours or more.
Can I request a specific braider?
You book the style and time that work for you, and the next available braider takes care of you. Send or bring a photo of the style you want, and we'll match you with the braider who does it best. If you have a sensitive scalp or want lighter tension, say so in the booking notes.
Do you braid kids' hair?
Yes, for kids age 5 and up.
Can I walk in?
Yes, whenever a braider is free. Booking online guarantees your time.
How do I pay?
The deposit is paid by card online when you book, and it comes off your final price. You pay the rest in cash, Zelle or Cash App at the salon. We don't take cards at the salon.
