How booking works

No back-and-forth messages. Book any time, day or night.

  1. Pick your style and timeSee open times for today and the coming weeks.
  2. Pay a small deposit$30–$50 holds your spot and comes off your final price.
  3. Get a reminderWe text and email you before your appointment. Reply to confirm or reschedule.
