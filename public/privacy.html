Privacy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Booking

Online booking and deposits are handled by Square Appointments; the booking form on our home page is Square's own page, shown inside our site. When you book, Square collects your name, phone number, email and payment details so it can confirm your appointment, send reminders and take your deposit. See Square's privacy notice.

Text messages

Appointment confirmations and reminders are sent to the phone number you give when you book. We only send promotional texts if you agree to them, and you can reply STOP at any time to stop them.

Measuring our ads

This website may use Google and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) tools to measure whether our ads lead to visits, calls and booking clicks. These tools may use cookies or similar technology. You can control ad personalization in your Google and Meta settings.

Map

The map on our home page is provided by Google Maps. It loads only when you scroll to it, and Google's privacy policy applies to it.
